## 🏛️ **CAMPUS ADMINISTRATOR ARCHITECTURE**

### **Four-Portal Hierarchical System**:
```
Supreme Admin Portal (Platform Level)
    ↓ Manages & Oversees
Campus Administrator Portal (University Level) - NEW
    ↓ Campus Jurisdiction
Owner Portal (Property Level)
Student Portal (User Level)
```

### **Authority Structure**:
- **Supreme Admin**: Ultimate platform authority, manages all Campus Admins
- **Campus Admin**: University-specific management, reports to Supreme Admin
- **Owners**: Managed by Campus Admin of their jurisdiction
- **Students**: Verified and managed by their Campus Administrator
