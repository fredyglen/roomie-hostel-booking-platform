STOREY BUILDING : Storey building have floors, and on every floor of the storey building have rooms, this could be even rooms on that floor or not, and could be again, even rooms  across all floors or not even across all floors at the same time. now within each room are beds provided. and these can be up to 8 depending on the size of the room. but the dominant once found in purposely build student hostels are from 1 bed up to 4 beds. and this introduces a term "1 in a room, or 2 in a room, or 3 in a room. you could see the trend. (the number of beds in a room). so these are what property owners track for thier money. depeneing on the property and its amenities and facilities, a bed could be reserved for whatever amount they choose to. but here's the catch, the lesser the number of beds in a room, the much pricey the bed becomes. so you'll get to realize that 4 in a room is less affordable that 1 in a room. which at this point I think you'll understand. now, with a storey typ property which has about 7 floors, can have exact number of rooms with exact same number of everything it offers, thus, amenities and features, and in this case, its not shared. on the other hand, the same property cound have the same 7 floors, but then lets say, 10 rooms on the ground floor, and within thise ground floor, each room can have 4 beds, but there are cases where about 2 out of these 10 rooms wont have 4 beds as all the rest do, but rather, may offer another set of beds. continuing from there, the first floor could have about 9 rooms, and again, have different number of beds offered across these rooms. and it is so across all floors, its either constant number of rooms and beds with its amenities, or randomized number of rooms, beds and amenities provided.   and  AN EXAMPLE IS THAT, A PROPERTY WITH 4 FLOORS, COULD HAVE 8 ROOMS ON THE GROUND FLOOR, AND WITHIN THAT 8 ROOMS ON THE GROUND FLOOR, MIGHT HAVE 4 BEDS IN 6 ROOMS (WHICH IS THE UNIT WE'RE TRACKING FOR RESERVATION: BEDS) BUT WILL HAVE 1 BED IN ANOTHER ROOM, AND THEN HAVE 2 BEDS IN THE LAST ROOM ON THE SAME GROUND FLOOR.
