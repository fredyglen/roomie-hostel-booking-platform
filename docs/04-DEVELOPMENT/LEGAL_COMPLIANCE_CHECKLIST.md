# ⚖️ ROOMie Legal & Compliance Documentation Checklist

## 📋 OVERVIEW

This document outlines ALL legal and compliance documents required before launching ROOMie platform in Ghana. Each document is prioritized and includes key sections that must be covered.

---

## 🚨 CRITICAL DOCUMENTS (Must Complete Before Launch)

### **1. Terms and Conditions**

**Priority:** 🔴 **CRITICAL**  
**Timeline:** Complete 2 weeks before launch  
**Purpose:** Legal agreement between ROOMie and all users (students, owners, agents, admins)

**Key Sections Required:**

1. **Acceptance of Terms**
   - By using ROOMie, users agree to these terms
   - Age requirement (18+ or parental consent)
   - Right to modify terms with notice

2. **User Accounts & Registration**
   - Account creation requirements
   - User responsibilities for account security
   - Prohibited activities (fraud, misrepresentation)
   - Account suspension/termination conditions

3. **Platform Services**
   - Description of ROOMie services
   - No guarantee of property availability
   - Platform is intermediary, not property owner
   - Right to refuse service

4. **Booking & Payment Terms**
   - Booking process and confirmation
   - Payment obligations and methods
   - Refund and cancellation policy (reference separate policy)
   - Commission structure transparency

5. **Property Listings**
   - Owner/agent responsibilities for accuracy
   - Verification process and standards
   - Right to remove non-compliant listings
   - Intellectual property rights for photos/content

6. **Liability Limitations**
   - Platform not liable for property conditions
   - Not liable for disputes between students and owners
   - Force majeure clause
   - Maximum liability cap

7. **Dispute Resolution**
   - Mediation process
   - Arbitration clause (Ghana jurisdiction)
   - Governing law (Ghana)

8. **Intellectual Property**
   - ROOMie owns platform IP
   - User-generated content license
   - Trademark usage restrictions

9. **Termination**
   - User right to terminate account
   - Platform right to terminate for violations
   - Effect of termination on active bookings

10. **Miscellaneous**
    - Entire agreement clause
    - Severability
    - No waiver
    - Contact information for legal notices

**Ghana-Specific Considerations:**
- Compliance with Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)
- Reference to Ghana Alternative Dispute Resolution Act, 2010 (Act 798)
- Jurisdiction: Courts of Ghana

---

### **2. Privacy Policy**

**Priority:** 🔴 **CRITICAL**  
**Timeline:** Complete 2 weeks before launch  
**Purpose:** Explain how ROOMie collects, uses, stores, and protects user data

**Key Sections Required:**

1. **Information We Collect**
   - Personal information (name, email, phone, student ID)
   - Payment information (handled by Paystack)
   - Property information (for owners/agents)
   - Usage data (analytics, cookies)
   - Location data (for property search)

2. **How We Use Your Information**
   - Facilitate bookings and payments
   - Verify student status
   - Communicate with users
   - Improve platform services
   - Comply with legal obligations
   - Prevent fraud and abuse

3. **Data Sharing & Disclosure**
   - With property owners (student booking info)
   - With payment processors (Paystack)
   - With service providers (Supabase, Vercel)
   - With law enforcement (when required)
   - Never sell personal data to third parties

4. **Data Security**
   - Encryption in transit and at rest
   - Secure authentication (Supabase Auth)
   - Regular security audits
   - Employee access controls
   - Incident response procedures

5. **Data Retention**
   - How long we keep data
   - Deletion upon account closure
   - Legal retention requirements
   - Backup and archival policies

6. **User Rights (Ghana Data Protection Act)**
   - Right to access personal data
   - Right to correct inaccurate data
   - Right to delete data (with exceptions)
   - Right to data portability
   - Right to object to processing
   - How to exercise these rights

7. **Cookies & Tracking**
   - Types of cookies used
   - Purpose of each cookie
   - How to disable cookies
   - Impact of disabling cookies

8. **Third-Party Services**
   - Supabase (database & auth)
   - Paystack (payments)
   - Vercel (hosting)
   - Sentry (error monitoring)
   - Aptabase (analytics)
   - Links to their privacy policies

9. **Children's Privacy**
   - Platform not intended for under-18 without consent
   - Parental consent requirements
   - How we handle minors' data

10. **International Data Transfers**
    - Data stored in Supabase (AWS regions)
    - Adequate safeguards for transfers
    - User consent for transfers

11. **Changes to Privacy Policy**
    - How users will be notified
    - Effective date of changes
    - Continued use implies acceptance

12. **Contact Information**
    - Data Protection Officer contact
    - How to submit privacy requests
    - Response timeline (30 days)

**Ghana-Specific Compliance:**
- Align with Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)
- Register with Data Protection Commission (Ghana)
- Appoint Data Protection Officer if required

---

### **3. Refund & Cancellation Policy**

**Priority:** 🔴 **CRITICAL**  
**Timeline:** Complete 2 weeks before launch  
**Purpose:** Clear rules for booking cancellations and refunds

**Key Sections Required:**

1. **Student Cancellation Rights**
   - Cancellation before move-in date
   - Cancellation after move-in date
   - Emergency cancellations (medical, academic)
   - Refund timelines and amounts

2. **Property Owner Cancellation**
   - When owners can cancel bookings
   - Penalties for owner cancellations
   - Student compensation for owner cancellations

3. **Refund Processing**
   - Refund calculation method
   - Platform fee refund policy
   - Payment processing fee (non-refundable)
   - Refund timeline (7-14 business days)
   - Refund method (original payment method)

4. **Non-Refundable Fees**
   - Platform fee (5% + GHS 100) - partial refund only
   - Payment processing fees
   - Agent commission (if booking confirmed)

5. **Dispute Resolution**
   - How to dispute a cancellation
   - Mediation process
   - Final decision authority

6. **Force Majeure**
   - Natural disasters, pandemics, government actions
   - Refund policy in force majeure situations
   - Rescheduling options

**Example Refund Schedule:**
```
Cancellation Timeline | Refund Amount
---------------------|---------------
30+ days before      | 90% refund
15-29 days before    | 70% refund
7-14 days before     | 50% refund
Less than 7 days     | 25% refund
After move-in        | No refund (unless emergency)
```

## 🇬🇭 GHANA-SPECIFIC LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

### **Regulatory Compliance:**

1. **Data Protection Commission Registration**
   - Register as data controller
   - Annual compliance reporting
   - Appoint Data Protection Officer (if required)

2. **Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA)**
   - Register for TIN (Tax Identification Number)
   - VAT registration (if applicable)
   - Monthly/quarterly tax filings

3. **Registrar General's Department**
   - Business name registration
   - Company incorporation (if not already done)

4. **National Communications Authority (NCA)**
   - Compliance with electronic communications regulations
   - If sending SMS notifications

5. **Bank of Ghana**
   - Compliance with payment regulations
   - Paystack handles most compliance
