# ROOMi Updated Business Model
## Based on Your Strategic Clarifications

## 💰 FINALIZED REVENUE STRUCTURE

### **PRIMARY REVENUE STREAMS**

#### **1. PROPERTY BOOKING COMMISSIONS**
- **Free Property Listing**: First property per owner/agent is free
- **Platform Commission**: **5% of successful booking value**
- **Platform Fee**: **100 GHS fixed fee per booking**
- **Example**: 3,000 GHS booking = 150 GHS commission + 100 GHS fee = **250 GHS total**

#### **2. PREMIUM STUDENT SUBSCRIPTIONS**
- **Credits-Based System**: Students buy ROOMi Credits for premium features
- **Flexible Spending**: Use credits when needed, not monthly recurring
- **Target Revenue**: 60,000+ GHS annually from premium features

#### **3. ADDITIONAL PROPERTY LISTINGS**
- **First Property**: Free listing
- **Additional Properties**: Listing fee per additional property
- **Suggested**: 50-100 GHS per additional property listing

## 💳 SHARED PAYMENT OPTIMIZATION

### **PRIMARY BOOKER SYSTEM**
**Challenge Solved:**
- **Paystack Fee Optimization**: Single 1.95% fee instead of multiple charges
- **Example**: 6 students sharing 6,000 GHS apartment
  - **Traditional**: 6 × 1.95% = 11.7% total fees (702 GHS)
  - **Primary Booker**: 1 × 1.95% = 1.95% total fees (117 GHS)
  - **Savings**: 585 GHS (83% reduction in payment fees)

### **IMPLEMENTATION PROCESS**
1. **Primary Booker Selection**: One student designated as payment coordinator
2. **Roommate Collection**: Primary booker collects shares from roommates
3. **Platform Payment**: Primary booker pays full amount to platform
4. **Booking Confirmation**: All roommates receive booking confirmation
5. **Shared Responsibility**: All roommates listed on booking agreement

### **VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS**
- **Student ID Verification**: All roommates must verify student status
- **Contact Information**: Phone numbers and emergency contacts required
- **Payment Confirmation**: Primary booker confirms roommate payments received
- **Booking Agreement**: All parties sign shared responsibility agreement

### **YOUR ROLE AS PAYMENT SYSTEM MANAGER**
- **Define primary booker selection criteria** and responsibilities
- **Approve roommate verification process** and requirements
- **Set shared payment policies** and dispute resolution procedures
- **Review booking agreement templates** and legal terms

2. **Campus Distance**: Proximity to university campus
3. **Property Availability**: Available beds and rooms
4. **Property Rating**: Student reviews and ratings
5. **Price Competitiveness**: Value for money assessment

**Search Result Ordering:**
```
🎯 PRIORITY 1: Properties within 2km of user location
```

## 📸 PROPERTY MEDIA STANDARDS

### **THREE-TIER MEDIA SYSTEM**

#### **TIER 1: COVER PHOTO**
- **Purpose**: Property card display image
- **Requirements**: High-resolution, well-lit, representative view
- **Specifications**: 1200×800 pixels minimum, JPEG/PNG format
- **Content**: Property exterior or most attractive feature

#### **TIER 2: INTERNAL MEDIA**
- **Purpose**: Room, facilities, and amenities documentation
- **Requirements**: Multiple angles, good lighting, accurate representation
- **Content**: Bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, common areas, amenities
- **Format**: Photos and videos, maximum 10MB per file

#### **TIER 3: ENVIRONMENT VIDEO**
- **Purpose**: Surrounding area and neighborhood overview
- **Requirements**: 2-5 minute walkthrough video
- **Content**: Property entrance, nearby facilities, transportation access
- **Quality**: 720p minimum, stable footage, clear audio
